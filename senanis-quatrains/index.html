Senanis Quatrains

Four moments, one stanza

A Senanis Quatrain brings four six-word Senanis Moments into a single stanza. Alternating end rhymes connect the lines as each moment offers its own image or turn.

Read more about the form of a Senanis Quatrain.

Senanis Quatrain

Going places

Thud, closing a once open door.

Emptiness ahead, in need of shove.

Patterns, amorphous, painting the ultimate floor.

Spring in step toward ceiling above.

Senanis Quatrain

A Fall Day

Feathered icebergs gently turning while asleep.

Tree’s complexion marching toward dusk’s red.

Leaves falling, finding one to keep.

Only bright blue above my head.

Senanis Quatrain

The Printer

Begin scan, but met with error.

Support not here, away on break.

Reset and pray, oh the terror.

Desire to scan, career at stake.
