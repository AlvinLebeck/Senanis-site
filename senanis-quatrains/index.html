Senanis Quatrains

Four moments, one stanza

A Senanis Quatrain brings four six-word Senanis Moments into a single stanza. Alternating end rhymes connect the lines as each moment offers its own image or turn.

Read more about the form of a Senanis Quatrain.

Going places

Thud, closing a once open door.

Emptiness ahead, in need of shove.

Patterns, amorphous, painting the ultimate floor.

Spring in step toward ceiling above.

A Fall Day

Feathered icebergs gently turning while asleep.

Tree’s complexion marching toward dusk’s red.

Leaves falling, finding one to keep.

Only bright blue above my head.

The Printer

Begin scan, but met with error.

Support not here, away on break.

Reset and pray, oh the terror.

Desire to scan, career at stake.

Second Glance

A warm breeze brushes her hair.

While many struggle to not look.

With ease she avoids their stare.

Eyes downcast, back to the book.

Dress flowing, might be a skirt.

We’ll never know, she’s hiding clues.

Eyes deceive, it’s a long T-shirt.

A belt and such colorful shoes.

Onward she goes smiling at passerby.

A nod or glance, recognition perhaps.

Several she graces with a “Hi.”

World rocked, oh the poor saps.

Carefully eyeing herself in the window.

Keeping it cool in summer’s heat.

Past cafes and bakeries she’ll forgo.

Stride nonchalant, not missing a beat.

Afternoon Calm

It’s my favorite time of day.

A quiet break, rest not play.

Neither early nor late, mid afternoon.

A deep calm breath coming soon.

Tap open to fill the pot.

Temperature set, make sure it’s hot.

Dried leaves assembled in small heap.

Now I must let it steep.

Phone timer set, tech’s little favor.

Duration determined to extract max flavor.

Now the wait, seems like forever.

I have a routine that’s clever.

Gather some snacks before timer dings.

My favorite chair’s siren now sings.

A mind swirling now slowly clears.

The rest of the day disappears.

Paperwork

Gather the papers ready to go.

Missing piece says it’s not so.

Told to return after we’ve dozed.

Only to find office is closed.

Everything set for return next day.

Approval granted but not available today.

Back home we go returning tomorrow.

It’s closed again, imagine our sorrow.

Completing this step, surely this time.

Further delays seem like a crime.

On to the office we ran.

Out for holiday, only one man.

Expectations

Now’s good before time passes by.

Help’s lending hand with family nearby.

Anxiously waiting the strip turns blue.

Calendar says a long winter’s due.

Belly expanding we haven’t a clue.

Imaging now with cool spreading goo.

Nurse casually says two upon arrival.

Staring blankly I question our survival.

Height barely more than her girth.

Struggling to last until their birth.

Eating for three banished to bed.

Important to ensure that she’s fed.

Pack a bag, set to go.

Precise day they don’t really know.

Clearly not wanting to risk chance.

We were ready months in advance.

The planned day is finally here.

Our quiet demeanor camouflages our fear.

Sleep comes easy we feel fine.

Late night puddle, I’m calling hotline.

It’s only hours before scheduled time.

Nurse says not rushing no crime.

Future mom eager to unleash superpower.

I decide to take quick shower.

Lost and Found

Silently rolls down the quiet street.

Unseen force pushes the tempo’s beat.

Thumping sounds when gathered herds disperse.

Forward only since there’s no reverse.

Horrified cries to begin a search.

Startled robin sings upon its perch.

Sifting arid furrows yields no fruit.

Steadfast they remain in hot pursuit.

Joyful shrieking portends a mad dash.

Fearful screaming tires screeching loud crash.

Staggered steps gasping before they fall.

Smiling widely emerging with the ball.

A Day to Save

All knowing extends far beyond pale.

Sweeping up all in pre-winter’s gale.

Unknowing influence, its whispers are spiritual.

Faithful partake in sacred annual ritual.

All routes follow same congested path.

Eschewing trek risks false idol’s wrath.

For nonbelievers the cost is despair.

True believers are said to compare.

At week’s end, see darkness descend.

Forces compel, treacherous heights to ascend.

Navigating mazes with many so brave.

Give thy soul, oh to save.

Upstairs

Almost not quite a pitter patter.

When first heard blood quickly fled.

Listening to silence afraid to scatter.

A constant knock above my head.

Rat or cat, prefer the latter.

Perhaps it’s something to be fed?

Not knowing sets nerves a tatter.

A constant knock above my head.

The elder’s cane prevents a splatter.

It’s now become a daily dread.

Does walking about cause the clatter?

A constant knock above my head.

Source unknown it should not matter.

I lay awake upon my bed.

Nightly calm it’s sure to shatter.

A constant knock above my head.
