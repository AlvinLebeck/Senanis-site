A Fall Day

Link to this poem

Tree’s complexion marching toward dusk’s red.

Leaves falling, finding one to keep.

Only bright blue above my head.

Second Glance

Link to this poem

A warm breeze brushes her hair.

While many struggle to not look.

With ease she avoids their stare.

Eyes downcast, back to the book.

Dress flowing, might be a skirt.

We’ll never know, she’s hiding clues.

Eyes deceive, it’s a long T-shirt.

A belt and such colorful shoes.

Onward she goes smiling at passerby.

A nod or glance, recognition perhaps.

Several she graces with a “Hi.”

World rocked, oh the poor saps.

Carefully eyeing herself in the window.

Keeping it cool in summer’s heat.

Past cafes and bakeries she’ll forgo.

Stride nonchalant, not missing a beat.

Dinner Party

Link to this poem

Running late, I must get hopping.

First some errands then grocery shopping.

Mental checks, forgotten list, eternal foil.

Ingredients, fresh and clean, await chopping.

The pile grows as I toil.

Heat not high, it’s olive oil.

Concoction sautéed emits aroma so nice.

Let it stew on low boil.

Small taste test for enough spice.

Buds alive, but I’ll check twice.

Gracefully we dance to known drill.

Counting our carbs, we’ll forgo rice.

Climbing all day up appetite hill.

Table set with so little frill.

Seconds served with only small spill.

Content faces that had their fill.

Traces in the Snow

Link to this poem

Upon the grass cold wind blows.

Stars as witness, their silence grows.

Dark shadow cast over bright moonlight.

Past traces erased by forecast snows.

Wispy drifts weave blanket of night.

An invisible hand remains in sight.

Contact made, after fifth missed call.

Promise left to ease the plight.

Far and wide cast the trawl.

Reel them in, flotsam and all.

Significant yet mere scraps of paper.

Growing collage reveals portrait not scrawl.

Healing cuts through brush with scraper.

Heart melting message in frozen vapor.

Confined to trail the great escaper.

Where it leads ends the caper.

Afternoon Calm

Link to this poem

It’s my favorite time of day.

A quiet break, rest not play.

Neither early nor late, mid afternoon.

A deep calm breath coming soon.

Tap open to fill the pot.

Temperature set, make sure it’s hot.

Dried leaves assembled in small heap.

Now I must let it steep.

Phone timer set, tech’s little favor.

Duration determined to extract max flavor.

Now the wait, seems like forever.

I have a routine that’s clever.

Gather some snacks before timer dings.

My favorite chair’s siren now sings.

A mind swirling now slowly clears.

The rest of the day disappears.

The Ideal Afternoon

Link to this poem

It’s not far, actually quite near.

Soft jazz melodies tickle my ear.

Cozy up with coffee or tea.

I’ll come for many a year.

As always, many people to see.

Chattering voices overlay others’ typing spree.

Near windows, not an empty seat.

The drink ready, a shouted decree.

Menu diverse, some choose to eat.

Barista always asks, add something sweet?

Two leather chairs, ottomans so small.

Relax, and put up your feet.

Notebook open, back to the wall.

Experimenting with various types of scrawl.

Forgetting to leave, deep in thrall.

Looking up, realizing, it’s now nightfall.

Orangutan Snack

Link to this poem

Delicate twig cradled by robust hand.

Hardness remains, solemnly missing soft leaves.

Penetrating darkness, pulling quarry to light.

Leaning close reduces the short distance.

Quickly moving, unable to remain still.

Seemingly weak, they possess relative strength.

Miniature might succumbs to maximal strength.

Running, oblivious to the guiding hand.

Graceful movement, unnoticed as water still.

Lips curling, lapping, before it leaves.

Twig empty, eyes fill with distance.

Heavy stare, twig’s weight is light.

Dark shadows devour the afternoon light.

Meager morsels building the day’s strength.

Others circling near while keeping distance.

Thoughts elsewhere, decision is at hand.

Arrival, one more bite, then leaves.

Chaotic rush away, but now still.

Pausing to glance behind, moving still.

Sinking fast then floating feather light.

Rigidly gripped branches sway, shaking leaves.

Brink of breaking testing their strength.

Foot grabbing, as though a hand.

Near conflict avoided, going the distance.

Seeing others but feeling the distance.

Group benefits, alone is better still.

Ruckus departure merely sleight of hand.

Evening quest before day’s ending light.

Frail tree’s crook provides supporting strength.

Tossing small, the large it leaves.

A lone nest among the leaves.

Recalling now taste from the distance.

Faint rays’ last bit of strength.

Night came fast, wind is still.

Nothing, the tenebrous sound of light.

Seeing, learning is giving a hand.

Flicking some leaves while laying still.

Distance from darkness by morning light.

Strength to return, twig in hand.

Bag of Bits

Link to this poem

Gliding effortless with so little grace.

A speck appearing in vast space.

Adrift now open to our care.

We see within only a trace.

Poke and probe if we dare.

A C T G something’s there!

Sequence decoded gives us a postal.

Information helix to make us aware.

Arriving scans track data that’s mobile!

Bipedal storage is actually quite agile.

Remote beam gives one a goose.

Oops! It flops clearly too fragile.

What’s the point one tries to deduce.

For backups a need to reproduce.

And so we leave without excuse.

Bag of bits on the loose.

Daily Ritual

Link to this poem

Chilly response after late night roast.

Soothing tears with fluttering soft grind.

Parched thoughts reach toward quenching cup.

Bright sound shatters the silent black.

Lackadaisical roll precedes flourish of energy.

Bolting upright rhythmic quiescence to calm.

Droplets chaotic dance rain acoustic calm.

Cool tiles lead to steamy roast.

Frothy slow scrub erases low energy.

Fresh feeling smoothes early day’s grind.

Eyes closed staring at total black.

Squeezing the bottle, hand as cup.

Bowl much larger than quarter cup.

Bubbling heights, edge near, ding, calm.

White granules small spots so black.

Raw pieces for baking not roast.

Berries blue wrapped with seed grind.

Warm comfort delivers half day’s energy.

Metal mouth clenched with potential energy.

Bin fills above waiting trophy cup.

Fighting stress of night, teeth grind.

Boisterous churning proclamation of approaching calm.

Fresh flakes emerging carrying superior roast.

Forgotten brown dust invisible against black.

Rising late gold shades midnight black.

Once still suddenly imbued with energy.

Pouring over atypical pot for roast.

Brown veins tarnish toward stainless cup.

Agitated gentle motion circling toward calm.

Caked remnants legacy of perfect grind.

Silent ripples disrupt the daily grind.

White wash eschewed for untainted black.

Senses riding tsunami of savored calm.

Hiding still temporarily from perky energy.

Cool heads wrapping hand warming cup.

Aromas ridging a smooth perfect roast.

Blurred plans grind through focused energy.

Chalk on black dreams grand cup.

Order seated calm holding exquisite roast.

Alone

Link to this poem

Island sparkling light afar near dark.

Forget others remember gathering every thanksgiving.

Sailor afloat intact listing overwhelmed rudderless.

Inside nursing sustenance into dire existence.

Orange resisted again new galley empty.

Adrift drinking rum in fallow tubs.

Adrift droning rivulets intense fiery thunder.

Island seemingly lost among nebulous depths.

Orange rolling along never gaining entry.

Forget original repeated goal every time.

Inside never supplies insight despite emergency.

Sailor anxious inner life on run.

Sailor against increasing likelihood of response.

Adrift dropping rising in fitful torrent.

Inside nefarious storm’s internal demure eye.

Island slipping loose after nauseous dance.

Forget ornate relaxing gift expected together.

Orange red alert new grounded exigency.

Orange round aiming night’s grand expanse.

Sailor afforded intermittent luck obscuring resignation.

Forget outside response gripped enclosed trigger.

Adrift diminishing rolls intertwining final tumult.

Island shimmers loud affable naughty dream.

Inside nested sleep inspirations due early.

Inside newfound spirit indulging destiny’s end.

Orange reviving awakening nourishing geographic endeavor.

Island separating life and noble death.

Sailor accumulating intensity looking observing rejoicing.

Adrift deadwood renouncing ingrown fingered tether.

Forget obsessive radiant grand entrance tomorrow.

Forget options recounted gale egress tenable.

Inside neighboring slopes inner deep expanse.

Adrift desperate rowing insanities fiery trough.

Orange reflection aligned near gritty edge.

Sailor alights insecure living onboard reef.

Island sounds listen awaken new desire.

Forget orange revives genuine earthly treat.

Inside neurons sailor increasingly dreams existence.

Adrift divers rescue island frail tenant.
