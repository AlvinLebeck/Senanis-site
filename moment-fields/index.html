Senanis Moment Fields

Shared structures of alignments

A Senanis Moment Field is a finite structure formed from six Senanis Moments. Together, they create a shared space of alignment. Words can be re-aligned to form new Senanis Moments; no words are added or removed, only their associations change.

Each field stands alone, inviting exploration through interaction.
