Alone

Island sparkling light afar near dark.

Forget others remember gathering every thanksgiving.

Sailor afloat intact listing over rudderless.

Inside nursing sustenance into dire existence.

Orange resisted again new galley empty.

Adrift drinking rum in filthy tubs.

Adrift droning rivulets intense fiery thunder.

Island seemingly lost among nebulous depths.

Orange rolling along never gaining entry.

Forget original repeated goal every time.

Inside never supplies insight despite emergency.

Sailor anxious inner life on run.

Sailor against increasing likelihood of response.

Adrift dropping rising in fitful torrent.

Inside nefarious storms internal demure eye.

Island slipping loose after nauseous dance.

Forget ornate relaxing gift expected together.

Orange red alert new grounded exigency.

Orange round aiming night’s grand expanse.

Sailor afforded intermittent luck or resignation.

Forget outside response get everything together.

Adrift diminishing rolls intertwining final tumult.

Inside newfound spirit indulging destiny’s end.

Orange reviving awareness noiseless geographic endeavor.

Island separating life and noble death.

Sailor accumulating intensity looking observing rejoicing.

Adrift deadwood renouncing ingrown fingered tether.

Forget obsequious reasoned grand entrance tomorrow.

Forget options recounted gyrating exactly toward.

Inside neighboring sides inner deep expanse.

Adrift desperate rowing insanities fiery trough.

Orange round aligned near gritty edge.

Sailor alights insecure living onboard reef.

Island sounds listen awaken new desire.

Forget orange revives genuine earthly treat.

Inside neurons sailor increasingly dreams existence.

Adrift divers rescue island featured tenant.
