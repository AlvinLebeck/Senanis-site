Senanis Sphere Notes

How the Senanis Sphere holds together

The Senanis Sphere gathers twenty six-word Senanis Moments into a surface that can be explored from many directions. Each Moment occupies a hexagonal face, and a turn of the sphere can bring a different Moment or connection into view.

Sixty unique words rest at the vertices of the structure. When two Moments meet along an edge, they share the two words at either end of that edge. These shared two-word bonds connect neighboring Moments without fixing a single order in which the surface must be read.

Each face is itself a Senanis Hexagon. Its six words can be read from any starting point and in either direction, opening twelve possible readings of the same Moment.

The surface is inspired by fullerene geometry. Twelve pentagonal openings interrupt the field of hexagons, allowing it to curve into a sphere rather than continue as a flat honeycomb.

One continuous thread also moves through all sixty words. Mathematically, it is a Hamiltonian path: it visits every vertex exactly once. Following the thread reveals the complete poem, while exploring the faces allows its smaller Moments to emerge in an order chosen by the reader.

The structure holds these ways of reading together. A word can belong to a Moment, connect neighboring Moments, and take its place in the longer poem without losing any of those relationships.
