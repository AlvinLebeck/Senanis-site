Where will six words take you?

A personal space for exploring ideas that blend writing, technology, and reflection.

A Senanis Moment is a fleeting thought or observation distilled into six words — a glimpse of life and an invitation to pause.

Drawn from senary (six), manan (reflection), and -is (one who does), the word senanis expresses the idea that six words can hold depth, meaning, and resonance beyond their brevity.

Senanis is a practice of reflection, shaped by attention and constraint. Each Senanis Moment is both a glimpse outward, a reflection of the world, and a turning inward, an opportunity to reflect for yourself.

Across its different poetic forms, a Senanis Moment may be arranged, realigned, read in sequence, carried through a pattern of returning words, or traced along a path. Each form opens another way to notice what six words can hold.
