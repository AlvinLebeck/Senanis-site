Senanis Sestina

Daily Ritual

Chilly response after late night roast.

Soothing tears with fluttering soft grind.

Parched thoughts reach toward quenching cup.

Bright sound shatters the silent black.

Lackadaisical roll precedes flourish of energy.

Bolting upright rhythmic quiescence to calm.

Droplets chaotic dance rain acoustic calm.

Cool tiles lead to steamy roast.

Frothy slow scrub erases low energy.

Fresh feeling smoothes early day’s grind.

Eyes closed staring at total black.

Squeezing the bottle, hand as cup.

Bowl much larger than quarter cup.

Bubbling heights, edge near, ding, calm.

White granules small spots so black.

Raw pieces for baking not roast.

Berries blue wrapped with seed grind.

Warm comfort delivers half day’s energy.

Metal mouth clenched with potential energy.

Bin fills above waiting trophy cup.

Fighting stress of night, teeth grind.

Boisterous churning proclamation of approaching calm.

Fresh flakes emerging carrying superior roast.

Forgotten brown dust invisible against black.

Rising late gold shades midnight black.

Once still suddenly imbued with energy.

Pouring over atypical pot for roast.

Brown veins tarnish toward stainless cup.

Agitated gentle motion circling toward calm.

Caked remnants legacy of perfect grind.

Silent ripples disrupt the daily grind.

White wash eschewed for untainted black.

Senses riding tsunami of savored calm.

Hiding still temporarily from perky energy.

Cool heads wrapping hand warming cup.

Aromas ridging a smooth perfect roast.

Blurred plans grind through focused energy.

Chalk on black dreams grand cup.

Order seated calm holding exquisite roast.
