Senanis Sestina

Stolen Goods

Unpacking a few from his box.

We should’ve known one is black.

This just one favorite tee shirt.

Another has color close to red.

In with laundry, including mom’s skirt.

Dry then fold, into the drawer.

Sorted clothes go in dad’s drawer.

His clothes strewn in basket box.

Nicely folding an effort to skirt.

Soon the day turns to black.

Before it does, skies so red.

Then slide into a pajama shirt.

Warm morning calls for cool shirt.

Calmly digging through the makeshift drawer.

Never seeing the color near red.

Sure was taken from a box.

Hands move faster, searching for black.

Somewhere else they’ve seemed to skirt.

Mom did wash with her skirt.

Full load had many a shirt.

Included in haul were several black.

Could they be in dad’s drawer?

Gave him all from his box.

Still can’t find black or red.

Reflecting back, dad recalls seeing red.

Muddled thoughts swirl, maybe with skirt.

Certainly it’s not in the box.

Inspector’s skills to locate a shirt.

Once again we search dad’s drawer.

Aha! Hiding within stack of black.

Having just found the missing black.

Confident now dad has the red.

This time we check every drawer.

Even under the Christmas tree skirt.

On the hunt for slippery shirt.

Clues exhausted, we’re in a box.

Retracing steps black tee to skirt.

Rueful discovery of the red shirt.

Not dad’s drawer, in other box.
