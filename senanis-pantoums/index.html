Senanis Pantoums

Returning words, changing meanings

A Senanis Pantoum carries six-word moments through interlocking four-line stanzas. Repeated lines gather new meaning as their surroundings change, until the poem returns to its beginning.

Beyond Measure

Poverty could not suppress her wealth.

The world conspires against her plans.

Life unbound when in good health.

Skeptics abound, they’re no true fans.

The world conspires against her plans.

For some acts she shall absolve.

Skeptics abound, they’re no true fans.

Timidity no match for her resolve.

For some acts she shall absolve.

Of the essence, what’s been ginning.

Timidity no match for her resolve.

She has a habit, always winning.

Of the essence, what’s been ginning.

Life unbound when in good health.

She has a habit, always winning.

Poverty could not suppress her wealth.
