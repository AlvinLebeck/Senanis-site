Senanis Pantoums Notes

A thought returns, changed

A pantoum unfolds through interlocking four-line stanzas. The second and fourth lines of one stanza return as the first and third lines of the next. Each repeated line meets a new neighbor, inviting a different association or emphasis.

In a Senanis Pantoum, every line is a six-word Senanis Moment. Repetition allows those brief thoughts to deepen without changing their words.

In “Beyond Measure,” the final stanza brings back the opening stanza’s third line as its second line, and the opening line closes the poem. The ending returns to the beginning, carrying the experience of the intervening stanzas.
