About Senanis

Senanis is a creative project built around Senanis Moments: brief six-word reflections that invite attention, interpretation, and pause.

I created senanis as a way to explore how constraint can open meaning. A Senanis Moment is small, but not simple. Six words can hold an image, a feeling, a question, or more than one meaning at once. This site brings those Senanis Moments into different forms, including Momentle’s playful and reflective weekly arrangements, Senanis Moment Fields, Quantum Senanis Moments, and Senanis Sestinas.

I'm Alvin R. Lebeck. My work and interests span technology, ideas, and creative exploration, and Senanis grows from that intersection. It is a personal space for experimenting with reflection, language, structure, and interaction.

For a broader view of my personal and professional work, visit my homepage.
