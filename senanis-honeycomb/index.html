Senanis Honeycomb

Six-word paths through a wider field

Senanis Honeycombs can grow to different sizes by joining hexagons along shared edges. This Honeycomb contains seven hexagons. Begin at any word and follow connected, unvisited words until a six-word Senanis Moment emerges. This structure also produces complete readings through all twenty-four words. Other readings are possible.
