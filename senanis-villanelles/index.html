Senanis Villanelles

Two refrains, returning together

A Senanis Villanelle carries six-word moments through five three-line stanzas and a final four-line stanza. Two refrains alternate, returning together at the close.

Awake in the Night

The glowing clock staring at me.

No judgment on early or late.

Mind racing a little past three.

Attempt to catch just enough zee.

Guidance it’s said need about eight.

The glowing clock staring at me.

Busy thoughts bumble like a bee.

Cause unknown, perhaps what I ate.

Mind racing a little past three.

You slumber. I’m jealous of thee.

Matching your breaths isn’t my fate.

The glowing clock staring at me.

Checklist to finish, don’t you see.

Life serving a really full plate.

Mind racing a little past three.

It will come, accepting is key.

Fretting no more, I’ll simply wait.

The glowing clock staring at me.

Mind racing a little past three.
