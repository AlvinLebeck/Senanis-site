Senanis Villanelles Notes

Two thoughts that keep returning

A villanelle has nineteen lines: five three-line stanzas followed by a four-line stanza. Its first and third lines become refrains, alternating as the final lines of the next four stanzas. Both return together as the last two lines of the poem.

In a Senanis Villanelle, each line is a six-word Senanis Moment. The tercets follow an ABA rhyme pattern, and the closing quatrain follows ABAA. The refrains meet changing lines and contexts, allowing a thought to shift in feeling even as its words remain the same.

In “Awake in the Night,” the returning clock and racing mind echo the experience of sleeplessness. The final stanza moves toward acceptance while the two familiar thoughts remain.
